# What is asked at school: the Dutch primary curriculum, groep 3 to 8

Yobyn, 2026-10-06: "The look and feel looks good, now we need to focus on the
questions them self and the level of the questions, they need what is actually
asked in school." He chose the Dutch primary school (basisschool), groep 3 to
groep 8, ages 6 to 12, all of it.

This is the map the question generators follow. It is built from the SLO
tussendoelen rekenen-wiskunde (what a groep should be able to do), the moments
the Cito leerlingvolgsysteem tests at (M = midden, around January; E = eind,
June), the referentieniveaus 1F/1S that groep 8 ends on, and what the common
methods (Pluspunt, Wereld in getallen, Getal & Ruimte Junior) put in each
groep. Methods differ by a few weeks in when a topic arrives; the order does
not. **Yobyn: check it against what your children bring home, and tell us
where their school is ahead or behind.**

## How the game maps onto it

- **Grade N is groep N + 2.** Grade 1 = groep 3 (age 6, the first year of
  formal rekenen) … grade 6 = groep 8. Grades 7 to 10 are secondary school
  (klas 1 to 4) and keep the old generic questions until asked for. In Dutch
  the grade cards say "Groep 3" … "Groep 8", "Klas 1" … "Klas 4".
- **Difficulty is the moment in the school year.** Easy = the start of the
  year (B: last year's end, revisited). Medium = the middle (M). Hard = the
  end (E). A round mixes the topics of that moment, with the newest ones most
  often.
- **Notation as in the Dutch classroom:** × for keer, : for delen. Decimal
  comma (0,5) from groep 6, when decimals arrive.
- **Every answer goes on the keypad:** a whole number, or (from groep 6) a
  decimal. Remainders, fractions of amounts, percentages and measures are
  asked so that the answer is a number ("23 : 4 = 5 rest ?", "¾ van 20 = ?",
  "25% van 60 = ?", "2 m = ? cm").

**Money (geld)** is a strand of its own in groep 3 to 6 only (coins and
amounts from groep 3, amounts with a comma by the end of groep 6;
teaching/money.ts `teachesMoney`, done 2026-10-10). From groep 7 on money is
the setting of a sum, not the sum: "20% korting op €45", "3 pakken kosten
€6". Groep 7 and 8 and klas 1–4 are never asked to count coins.

## Klas 1–4 (age 12–16): the plan (2026-10-11; all four built by 2026-10-13)

Secondary school, onderbouw and the start of the bovenbouw: the kerndoelen
onderbouw (domein Getallen, Verbanden, Meten en meetkunde) and referentie-
niveau 2F/3F, in the order the common methods teach it (Getal & Ruimte,
Moderne Wiskunde). **Assumed stream: HAVO/VWO**, as Yobyn has not named
another; VMBO would move several topics a year later and leave out the
klas 4 algebra. Not a copy of any one method: each klas below lists what
every HAVO/VWO class meets that year, by moment.

What the keypad can take shapes how each is asked, as for the groepen: a
whole number, a decimal, or a negative number (the keypad has a minus
key). A fraction is answered as its numerator over a given denominator
(1/2 + 1/3 = ?/6), an equation as the value of x, a quadratic as one named
solution ("de grootste oplossing"). Nothing that needs a graph, a drawing
or words to answer. Klas 1 starts where groep 8 ended, and from klas 1 on
no round asks a times table or a division inside the tables on its own.

## Build order

One run each, in this order. Each one replaces the old generic questions for
its groep:

1. **Groep 3–4, the forms that are plain sums**: +/− within 10, 20 (without
   and with crossing the ten), tens, 100 (T±T, TE±E, TE±T, TE±TE), hundreds;
   the tables in the order they are learnt and dividing within them; × and :
   as written in class; the grade cards say groep 3 and 4. DONE in the first
   school-levels PR.
2. **The written forms of groep 3–4**: aanvullen and splitsen (7 + ? = 10,
   8 = 5 + ?), dubbel and helft, with the answer box where the "?" is.
   DONE in the second school-levels PR: aanvullen tot 10 from groep 3 M,
   splitsen tot 10 and dubbel/helft tot 20 at groep 3 E (revisited at
   groep 4 B), dubbel/helft tot 100 at groep 4 E. Not yet: aanvullen tot 20
   and to the next ten (34 + ? = 40), splitsen above 10.
3. **Groep 5**: all tables, +/− within 1000, times a ten, TE × E, remainder,
   part of an amount, measures. DONE in the third and fourth school-levels
   PRs: the tables of 6–9 and dividing within them (all ten tables known by
   the middle of the year), +/− to 1000 without going through a ten or a
   hundred (M) and through them (E), times a ten (M), TE × E (E), a third,
   a quarter or a fifth of an amount written ¼ van 20 (M), dividing with a
   remainder written 23 : 4 = 5 rest ? (E) and measures into a smaller
   unit, 3 m = ? cm, 2 km = ? m, 4 kg = ? g, 2 uur = ? minuten (E). Not
   yet: measures the other way (300 cm = ? m), litres.
4. **Groep 6**: +/− within 10 000, larger × and :, fractions of amounts,
   decimals (the comma on the keypad), area, rounding. FIRST HALF DONE in
   the fifth school-levels PR: +/− to 10 000 in columns, 25 × 8 and 96 : 4
   (M), TE × TE and rounding to tens and hundreds, written
   "Rond af op tientallen: 347 ≈ ?" (E). Fractions and area DONE in the
   sixth: ¾ van 20 and ½ = ?/8 (M), the area of a rectangle, 6 m × 4 m =
   ? m² (E). Decimals DONE in the seventh: 0,3 + 0,4, 1,5 + 2,7,
   2,4 − 0,8 with the comma on the card and the keypad (E). GROEP 6 IS
   COMPLETE.
5. **Groep 7**: big numbers, cijferend × and :, decimals × 10/100/1000,
   fractions, percentages, ratios, the metric system.
   WHOLE NUMBERS DONE in the eighth school-levels PR: rounding to
   thousands, 124 × 36, 864 : 24 and 50/25/10% van an amount (M).
   DECIMALS DONE in the ninth: × and : 10/100/1000 (M), a decimal times a
   whole number and the metric system with a comma (E). The rest DONE in
   the tenth: fractions with the same denominator, 2/8 + 3/8 = ?/8, and a
   fraction as a decimal, ¾ = 0,75 (M); ratios through the price of one,
   "3 pakken kosten €6, 7 pakken = €?", and discount, "20% korting: wat
   betaal je? €45 → €?" (E). GROEP 7 IS COMPLETE. Not yet: tenths and
   twelfths in 7/12 + 4/12 (too wide for one line of a small phone until
   a fraction is drawn over its line), mixed numbers.
6. **Groep 8**: percentages, scale, the mean, area and volume, time spans,
   speed, negative numbers. THE START AND THE MIDDLE ARE DONE (2026-10-10):
   B is groep 7's end (2,5 × 4, 2,5 km = ? m, pakken, korting, with 25% van
   60 and the staartdeling revisited). M: any percentage of an amount,
   worked through 10%, 5% or 1% ("15% van 60 = ?", kept as 3 × 3: 5% of 60
   is 3; "12% van 300" through 1%; never only the 10%, 25% or 50% of groep
   7); what percentage ("18 van 72 = ? %", kept as 1800 : 72, a whole
   percentage, never out of 100); bigger fractions of amounts ("⅔ van 45",
   up to 200); scale ("Schaal 1 : 500 / 4 cm = ? m", at 1 : 100, 200, 500
   and 1000, whole metres); the mean ("Wat is het gemiddelde? 11, 15, 10 →
   ?", kept as 36 : 3, the list made from the total and the count by
   `meanList`, always the same list for the same sum, all different and
   above nothing; four numbers are small ones, as 4, 8, 6, 10, since four of
   two figures and the box do not fit one line of a 320px phone). Each is
   worked out after two tries ("5% van 60 = 3 → 3 × 3 = 9", "11 + 15 + 10 =
   36 → 36 : 3 = 12"). Not yet: adding fractions with unlike denominators
   (the answer is a fraction, which the keypad cannot type until a fraction
   is drawn over its line). THE END IS DONE TOO (2026-10-10), AND GROEP 8 IS
   COMPLETE AND OPEN on the grade screen: the volume of a box ("Inhoud in
   cm³ / 10 × 5 × 4 = ?", kept as 50 × 4, the unit in the heading so the
   card fits a 320px phone); litres ("2 m³ = ? l", "3 l = ? cm³"); time
   spans ("Hoeveel minuten? / 9:45 → 11:20 ?", kept as 680 − 585 minutes
   from midnight, never on the hour, always past one, a school morning
   starting before 10:00 so both times fit one line; no "=" for the same
   reason); speed ("60 km per uur / 2,5 uur = ? km", kept as 5 half hours ×
   30, worked as 2 × 60 = 120 → 120 + 30 = 150); temperatures below nought
   ("Hoeveel graden verschil? / −3° → 5° = ?", up or down, kept as 5 + 3,
   worked through nought). The middle's topics are practised again at the
   end.
7. **Klas 1–4**: the plan above. Built klas by klas, each unlocking its

   OPEN (2026-10-12), kept in groep.ts as "groep 9": B is groep 8's end with

   worked line ("4 − 9 = 4 + −9 → 9 − 4 = 5 → −5", "4 × 5 = 20 → 3 + 20 =
   23"). Not yet: estimating (schatten), whose answer is not one number.
   KLAS 2 IS DONE AND OPEN (2026-10-12), "groep 10": B is klas 1's end; M
